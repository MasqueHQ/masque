How Masque works.

Robinhood Chain puts stock tokens onchain, where every wallet can be read by anyone. Masque is a terminal for those tokens with one goal: keep the market open and the holders private.

What Masque is

Today Masque is a non-custodial terminal. It reads Robinhood Chain directly in your browser and sends trades from your own wallet to Uniswap v3. Masque has no server holding funds and no contract of its own in the trading path.

Swapping

Pick a stock token, pay with ETH or USDG, and sign. Before your wallet opens, the terminal quotes every fee tier that has a pool, picks the best output, re-quotes right before signing and simulates the trade against the pool. Sells settle to USDG.

A swap is public. It comes from your own wallet, so anyone can read it on the explorer. Private holding comes with the vault.

Pools and prices

Each stock token trades in Uniswap v3 pools against USDG or ETH. The Pools tab lists every pool, what sits inside it and its value. The Swap tab shows two prices side by side: what the pool pays for one share, and the Chainlink reference price for the same stock. The gap between them is the cost of trading there right now.

Launches

The Launches tab reads the Pons factory's launch events and lists the newest tokens with their contract and creator. It is a feed, not a recommendation. Check every contract before you buy.

The vault

The vault is how holders become private. You deposit from the wallet you are known by. Your shares are then held at a fresh address only your key can open, and you withdraw to any address you pick. The deposit and the withdrawal are both on chain; what the vault removes is the public link between them.

The vault is planned. It opens only after an outside audit, with a small deposit cap at first. Nothing in Masque holds user funds today.

Perps

Leveraged positions will run on vault balances, so a position is not tied to the wallet you are known by. Until then, the Position lab lets you replay a leveraged position on real prices and see exactly where it would have been liquidated.

Risks

  • Stock tokens may not be available where you live. Check before you trade.
  • Pools can be thin. A large trade moves the price; the slippage limit protects you, the quote does not.
  • Smart contracts can fail. Masque uses the public Uniswap v3 contracts on Robinhood Chain and adds none of its own to the trade.
  • Tokens, $MASQ included, can lose all value.
